About Our System

The College Placement and Career Management System is a platform designed to make the placement process easier for students, companies, and the college placement team. It helps students find suitable job opportunities and provides companies with a convenient way to connect with talented students.

Our Features

Student Profile

Students can create and manage their academic and career information.

Job Opportunities

Students can explore available job and internship opportunities.

Company Management

Companies can share job openings and placement information.
